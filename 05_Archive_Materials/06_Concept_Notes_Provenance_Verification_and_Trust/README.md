## About the Series

These notes examine what happens when claims continue circulating after their sources become difficult to identify, inspect, or verify.

The series focuses on citation laundering, synthetic evidence, provenance loss, chain-of-custody failure, algorithmic authority, verification substitution, and the broader condition of Reality Drift.

The central question is not only whether a claim is accurate. It is whether the claim remains connected to evidence that can still be traced, challenged, and independently verified.
