## About the Series

These notes examine the representational structures through which knowledge systems classify entities, organize relationships, infer missing information, reconcile competing schemas, and determine what counts as true.

The central concern is not simply whether a knowledge system contains errors. It is whether the system remains capable of being corrected by the reality it was designed to represent.
