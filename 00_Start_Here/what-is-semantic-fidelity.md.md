## What Is Semantic Fidelity?

*A short introduction to the Semantic Fidelity framework.*

---

## Overview

Semantic Fidelity describes whether meaning survives as language moves through systems, models, compression, translation, and artificial intelligence.

Language increasingly passes through layers of mediation before reaching another person. AI models, digital platforms, institutional processes, and automated workflows all transform information while preserving the appearance of coherence.

When semantic fidelity is high, meaning remains intact across these transformations. When semantic fidelity degrades, language can remain accurate, fluent, and persuasive while gradually losing the intent, context, and significance it originally carried.

Semantic Fidelity provides a framework for understanding and studying this process.

---

## How Semantic Fidelity Degrades

Semantic Fidelity commonly weakens through three interacting processes.

### Compression

Complex ideas are reduced into simplified representations that preserve core information while losing nuance, context, and constraint.

### Recursive Transformation

Language is repeatedly summarized, translated, paraphrased, retrieved, and regenerated as it moves through people, institutions, and AI systems.

### Optimization Pressure

Systems increasingly optimize for fluency, coherence, engagement, or efficiency rather than preserving the original meaning.

Together, these processes can produce language that remains convincing while gradually becoming less faithful to what it was intended to communicate.

## Areas of Study

The Semantic Fidelity framework examines topics including:

- Meaning preservation
- Semantic compression
- AI-generated language
- Retrieval and summarization
- Interpretation failure
- Semantic grounding
- Recursive transformation
- Fidelity decay

## Relationship to Reality Drift

Semantic Fidelity represents the linguistic layer of the broader Reality Drift framework.

While Reality Drift examines how systems gradually lose alignment with the realities they are intended to represent, Semantic Fidelity examines whether meaning survives as information moves through those systems.
