# Semantic Fidelity Lab Archive

**A. Jacobs**  
*Archived Substack Essays (2025–2026)*

This repository preserves the original essays published under the **Semantic Fidelity Lab (SFL)** project between October 2025 and February 2026.

These texts document the early development of concepts related to semantic drift, semantic fidelity, recursive compression, constraint collapse, and meaning preservation in AI-mediated systems.

The essays are preserved in their original form with minimal archival framing for provenance, continuity, and long-term reference.

## Core Themes

Across these essays, several recurring themes emerge:

- **Semantic Fidelity** — preserving meaning across transformations

- **Semantic Drift** — gradual erosion of meaning under recursion

- **Fidelity Decay** — cumulative degradation of semantic structure

- **Meaning Debt** — long-term accumulation of semantic loss

- **Recursive Compression** — the structural mechanism behind drift

- **Constraint Collapse** — when feedback no longer forces correction

- **Synthetic Realness** — coherence without grounding

## Archival Note

These documents represent early exploratory work and are preserved as provenance artifacts.

Some concepts were later refined, expanded, or restructured in subsequent canonical work within the broader **Reality Drift framework**.

This archive exists to preserve conceptual lineage rather than establish final formulations.
