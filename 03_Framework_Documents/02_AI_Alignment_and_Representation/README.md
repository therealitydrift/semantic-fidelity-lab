# AI Systems and Reality Drift

A collection of short framework notes connecting AI evaluation, alignment, grounding, semantic fidelity, and organizational theory through the Reality Drift framework.

These papers explore a common question:

> How do systems continue functioning while gradually losing contact with the realities they were designed to represent?

Across AI systems, institutions, organizations, and information environments, many seemingly different failure modes share the same structural pattern. Representations remain active while their relationship to reality weakens.
