# Notes on Fidelity Decay

This directory contains research notes examining how meaning gradually degrades as language moves through repeated compression, transformation, and large-scale AI systems.

These papers focus on Fidelity Decay as a structural process, exploring how language can remain coherent and useful while progressively losing semantic richness, contextual constraints, and conceptual precision.

---

## Included Papers

### Measuring Fidelity Decay

Presents a framework for identifying and evaluating how meaning erodes across repeated transformations, introducing Fidelity Decay as a measurable property of language systems.

### Constraint Collapse and Fidelity Decay in Scaled Language Models

Examines how language models can continue producing fluent, coherent outputs after the constraints preserving semantic meaning have begun to weaken.

---

## Purpose

These papers investigate Fidelity Decay as a distinct mechanism within the broader Semantic Fidelity framework. They provide theoretical foundations for understanding how semantic degradation emerges across compression, recursive transformation, and generative AI systems.
