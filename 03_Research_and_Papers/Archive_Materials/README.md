# Supplementary Materials

## Supporting Research, Notes, and Reference Texts

### Overview

This directory contains supplementary materials related to the broader Semantic Fidelity Lab research ecosystem.

These materials support the development of the Semantic Fidelity framework but are not considered part of the core canonical infrastructure. They include exploratory essays, technical notes, and source materials that informed or contributed to the evolution of the broader research direction.

---

## Contents

### Essays Archive

Early exploratory essays, conceptual drafts, and archived written material related to semantic fidelity, representational systems, AI-mediated cognition, and recursive semantic environments.

These documents reflect earlier stages of conceptual development and experimental framing work.

---

### Source Texts

Reference materials, foundational works, and supporting intellectual sources relevant to the development of the Semantic Fidelity framework.

This folder contains contextual materials used for conceptual grounding, comparative analysis, and broader theoretical orientation.

---

### Technical Notes

Working notes, implementation concepts, structural observations, and intermediate technical material related to semantic fidelity systems and recursive AI environments.

These documents function primarily as supplemental research artifacts rather than finalized framework documents.
